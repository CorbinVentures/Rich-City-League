# RCL Network — Virginia Basketball Exposure Model

## Product position

RCL has two complementary roles:

1. **RCL Network** — the statewide discovery, media, audience, and exposure layer for Virginia basketball.
2. **Rich City League** — RCL's flagship competitive basketball property.

RCL Network exists to make Virginia basketball easier to discover, follow, share, and support. It is not intended to replace the operational software used by leagues, tournaments, clubs, programs, or teams.

## Non-negotiable scope

### RCL Network supports
- Organization discovery
- Official organization pages
- Event discovery and listings
- Content amplification
- Featured placements and sponsored promotion
- Regional and statewide audience distribution
- Media/editorial opportunities
- Audience and exposure analytics
- Outbound traffic to an organization's own website or registration destination

### RCL Network does not support
- Registration management
- Payment processing for partner organizations
- Roster management
- Scheduling administration
- League or tournament operations
- Team management
- Standings/stat administration for external organizations

External organizations keep ownership and control of their operations. RCL provides distribution and exposure.

## Brand architecture

### RCL Network
The statewide basketball network. Organizations may be competitors on the court while still participating in the same discovery ecosystem.

### Rich City League
The flagship RCL-owned league. Because RCL owns this property, it can receive deeper native integrations such as official game data, statistics, fantasy, rankings, highlights, and documentary coverage.

The flagship advantage comes from ownership and deeper data access—not from suppressing competing organizations in Network discovery.

## Organization tiers

### Network Listing — Free
Goal: maximize statewide coverage.

Core value:
- Official organization page
- Virginia regional/category discovery
- Website and social links
- Upcoming event listings
- Standard content visibility
- Verification eligibility

### Amplify — launch target $49/month
Goal: help organizations grow audience and event awareness.

Core value:
- Everything in Network Listing
- Enhanced organization discovery
- Regional/category featured placement inventory
- Up to 2 event spotlight placements per month
- Selected content amplification
- Exposure reporting: impressions, page views, event views, outbound clicks, shares, audience growth

### Premier Partner — launch target $149/month
Goal: provide a recurring multi-channel exposure partnership.

Core value:
- Everything in Amplify
- Up to 4 featured event placements per month
- Rotating statewide feature inventory
- Priority consideration for RCL editorial/media coverage
- Cross-channel campaign distribution when inventory is available
- Advanced exposure/campaign reporting
- Premier Partner identity on the organization page

Pricing is a launch hypothesis and should be validated against organization demand and measured traffic before being treated as permanent.

## Paid promotion rules

- Sponsored placements must be clearly labeled.
- Paying for exposure never changes competitive results, rankings, REP, awards, or editorial truth.
- Organic discovery remains available to free organizations.
- Partner status improves distribution inventory; it does not create fake basketball credibility.

## Exposure metrics

RCL should report outcomes that organization operators can understand:
- Impressions
- Organization page views
- Event views
- Outbound clicks
- Follower growth
- Shares
- Media views

The long-term sales story is measurable distribution, not vague promises to "promote" an organization.

## Trust principle

RCL Network should be useful even to organizations that compete directly with Rich City League. Participation should not require exclusivity or migration to RCL operational software.

The product promise is simple:

> Keep running your organization your way. RCL helps Virginia basketball find you.
