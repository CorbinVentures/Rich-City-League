# Rich City League — proposed 2027–28 competition rulebook
**Version:** 0.1 executive working draft · October 30, 2026  
**Status:** PROPOSED / NOT IN FORCE. No public enrollment, payment, player promise, draft right, team franchise, or match result may rely on this document until the Commissioner has approved and published a signed version.  
**Scope:** Rich City League (RCL), Draft Night, and Rich City Fantasy. RCH social/Runs/Commonwealth Cup are **separate** programs unless expressly incorporated by a season-specific rule.  
**Controlling calendar:** Official new professional season **planned** for **November 2027**; opening day, season end, schedule, and official tryout dates are **unconfirmed**. See `docs/RCL_PRO_SEASON_NOV_2027_POLICY.md`.

## 1. Purpose and hierarchy
RCL exists to provide fair, safe, consistently operated basketball competition and credible opportunities for qualified athletes. Sporting fairness, participant safety, and truthful business representations take priority over publicity or digital gamification.

1. Applicable law, signed player/operator agreements, and mandatory venue/sanctioning obligations prevail.
2. The **signed, published season rulebook** and its dated season appendices govern competition.
3. Authorized written commissioner bulletins may clarify rules prospectively but cannot retroactively alter finalized outcomes except through a documented protest, disciplinary appeal, or verified scoring correction.
4. This draft and website previews are informational. Social XP, REP, popularity, Runs ratings, badges and fantasy scores confer **no** player eligibility, roster rights, or draft preference.
5. Rule changes must be versioned with approver, effective date, impacted season, notice to clubs and participants, and a record of any grandfathered cases.

## 2. Governance and conflicts
- **League Commissioner**: competition authority, season approval, franchise participation oversight, and final decisions subject to published appeal procedures.
- **Basketball Operations**: eligibility verification, rosters, player rights register, draft, schedules, officials, and game operations.
- **Independent discipline/appeals panel**: reviews cases involving the Commissioner, an operator, or staff conflicts; recused parties do not vote.
- **Head scorekeeper and statistics reviewer**: certify official result and player statistics; neither may correct their own disputed entry without a second reviewer.
- **Finance**: cash receipts, restricted sponsor funds, settlements, insurance compliance, financial records and audit trail.
- No official may use nonpublic player evaluations, injury reports, draft decisions, or prepublication statistics to benefit a fantasy team, betting, sponsor, or affiliated club.

**Open approval:** Appoint real named roleholders, alternates, recusal rules and independent disciplinary reviewers before season activation.

## 3. League size and admission
Existing website team identities are not automatically admitted 2027–28 franchises. Admission is granted only after executed club/operator agreements and approved operating evidence.

**Pilot recommendation, not a final league decision:** six geographically sustainable clubs; expand to eight only if financing, venues, rosters and travel plans pass the same gates. The existing 12-team preseason draft preview does **not** by itself admit twelve clubs. Any new field size requires an approved revised order before live player selections.

Minimum club evidence:
- identifiable accountable operator; legal and insurance review; facilities and backup dates;
- funded season budget and reserve, payroll and travel responsibility;
- coach, authorized roster manager and gameday contacts;
- player agreement process, code-of-conduct acknowledgment, safeguarding and medical/emergency plan;
- consistent branding, sponsorship clearances, financial and statistical reporting.

Failure to meet requirements leads to pending, suspended or denied status; a commissioner must publish objective reasons and an appeal path.

## 4. Competition calendar and format
**Proposed pilot for approval:** six teams, double round robin (ten regular-season games per team, thirty league-wide), top four playoff with seeded semifinals and championship. No published date or venue is authorized by this suggestion. A future eight- or twelve-club schedule requires a distinct voted format and financial model.

Before opening night, publish:
- exact start/end dates, schedule, home/away allocation, divisions (if any) and geographic travel policy;
- game rules authority (NFHS/FIBA or an expressly adopted RCL code), quarter/overtime length, timing, timeout, substitution and foul standards;
- standings order: win percentage, head-to-head, then published tie-breakers; do not improvise after games begin;
- playoff eligibility, home court, overtime, match cancellation/reschedule, travel-disruption and weather procedures.

**No default game-rule code, point-differential tiebreakers, salary guarantees, or playoff format becomes binding merely because it appears in this proposal.**

## 5. Player eligibility and safeguarding
- **Proposed competition minimum:** 18 years of age on the first day of official season participation, pending counsel and insurance approval. This does not change the broader RCH social platform age policy.
- Verify identity, age, consent, roster eligibility, incompatible team commitments, disciplinary status and any other rules published before tryouts.
- Current college athletes receive an eligibility advisory and must consult their athletics compliance office before contracts, professional representation or prize/compensation arrangements.
- Tryout attendance alone does not create draft eligibility. Basketball Operations must explicitly approve each player's season-specific draft pool status.
- Maintain minimal restricted identity records and a correction/appeal path. Do not expose personal documents, private evaluations or medical histories to fans.

## 6. Player contracts and classification
RCL must decide and publish whether athletes are paid, how compensation is calculated, payment dates, work classification, insurance, medical coverage, appearance rights and release/transfer terms **before** issuing offers. The word `professional` does not determine legal classification or guarantee payment.

Written player acknowledgment must address:
- league/club relationship; term and termination; game availability and conduct;
- likeness and broadcast consent, media use and data rights;
- injuries, emergency response and return-to-play clearance;
- disputes and grievance process; any release and future rights restrictions.

No unpublished salary cap, reserve obligation or exclusive player-rights term is enforceable.

## 7. Rosters and acquisition
**Proposed pilot, subject to finance/safety approval:** 12-player maximum active season roster per club, with a documented minimum game-eligible number and medical/injury reserve designation fixed in a season appendix.

Every roster transaction needs authorized actor, season ID, player ID, team ID, effective timestamp, reason, contract status, and auditable approval. One player may not be simultaneously active on two RCL clubs in the same season.

Permitted acquisition channels must be explicitly approved:
1. official draft selections from the certified eligible pool;
2. retained or previously contracted players **only** if published protection rules establish those rights;
3. documented post-draft free-agent signing;
4. authorized releases, transfers or waivers under a published priority/claim procedure;
5. injury replacement with a specific authorization period.

Expansion and trade rights are **not** automatically implied. Transactions after registration/roster lock are prohibited except for published exceptions.

## 8. Draft Night
- The currently established four-round snake configuration is a **preseason allocation preview**, not a playable draft or player assignment.
- Only admitted clubs with approved season participation may select; order must be recalculated and formally approved if team membership changes.
- Draft opens only after official season creation, published rules, authorized staff, certified eligible player pool, sufficient draftable players, valid team slots and roster-capacity preflight.
- Draft settings (rounds, order, clock, roster cap) lock when official selection begins. Coaches may pick only for their assigned club while on clock. Every event must be written to the audit ledger.
- The published clock, timeout/pause policy, outage procedure, forfeited/missed picks, duplicate selections, trades, eligibility challenge, correction/replay procedure and appeals must be approved **before live activation**.
- The four-round draft **does not create complete 12-player rosters**. Post-draft acquisition rules and any protected/returning players must fill remaining places.
- No pick creates an athlete's binding obligation before the applicable agreement is executed and any acceptance prerequisites are met.

## 9. Game operations and safety
- A named event director checks venue permissions, court suitability, equipment, officials, first-aid/AED access, emergency transport route, roster eligibility and incident contacts before opening doors.
- Assign independent officials, scorekeeper, official clock operator, site lead and backup. Confirm the published officiating ruleset and uniforms before warmups.
- Player injury/medical decisions belong to competent medical personnel under the emergency plan, not fantasy managers or producers.
- No game begins without the minimum verified safety/competition personnel. Document postponement, cancellation and refund procedures in advance.
- Protect spectators, staff, opponents and venue personnel; impose sanctions for violence, abuse, harassment, discriminatory conduct, match manipulation or interference with officials.

## 10. Results, statistics and integrity
- Record scores and player statistics to a **season- and game-specific official Scorebook**. Require initial scorer certification plus independent reviewer approval before final publication.
- Preserve immutable original entries, revision reason, responsible editor, reviewer and effective timestamp; corrections never silently rewrite history.
- A published stat-correction window must specify when regular-game and fantasy results become final and what happens when a late verified error is discovered.
- Do not award wins, championships, player rankings, fantasy points or draft rights from self-reported Runs stats.
- Fantasy scoring must snapshot eligible lineups by official game before tipoff; later lineup edits cannot retroactively capture those points.
- System errors and contested game outcomes require formal exception logs and human review.

## 11. Discipline, protests and appeals
Proposed process (deadlines require ratification):
1. Officials record ejections, severe misconduct and game disruptions immediately.
2. Basketball Operations notifies affected club/player in writing with incident record and provisional status.
3. A game protest may be filed by the authorized club representative within **48 hours** of the recorded final, without publishing sensitive evidence.
4. The independent panel reviews evidence, permits a response, and issues a reasoned decision under a published timetable.
5. A person involved in an original decision does not adjudicate the appeal.
6. Emergency safeguarding sanctions may apply before a hearing; reasons, duration and review rights must be recorded.

Scorekeeping errors, eligibility protests and discipline appeals are distinct processes; deadlines and standing must be in the final appendix.

## 12. Club finance, travel, insurance and suspension
- Every club maintains a season operating budget, named funding responsibility, insurance certificates and payroll/tax process as applicable.
- League headquarters maintains its own season P&L, contingency reserve and cash-flow forecast.
- Venue, officials, player compensation, medical, security, broadcast, marketing, transportation and uniform obligations **must be assigned by contract**; never assume a percentage share identifies who pays.
- Unpaid obligations or repeated forfeits trigger documented cure notices, suspension criteria and orderly league emergency management.
- No operator may sell an RCL territory, team equity or guaranteed franchise returns on the basis of a preliminary revenue matrix.

## 13. Revenue and sponsorship
- The October 14 Franchise Owner Revenue Split Framework is a **nonbinding proposal**; it does not grant equity or territory rights.
- Proposed shares operate on separately defined **net distributable receipts**, not gross sales. No double-counting or unrelated overhead deductions.
- Legal counsel and finance must approve each category, cost codes, local-vs-central sale attribution, visiting-team split, sponsor restrictions, settlements, audit rights and dispute resolution before offers.
- Local sponsor claims and sponsor-category conflicts must clear league brand rights.
- Central media and league sponsor proceeds must be disclosed and allocated under the signed season schedule.
- Reconcile only **collected** receipts, not promises or unpaid invoices.

## 14. Media, intellectual property and athlete privacy
RCL controls league competition marks and central program IP subject to executed contracts. Local club name, logo, footage, game data, sponsorship deliverables and sublicensing rights require written schedules. Athlete likeness use must be licensed and privacy/guardian rules followed where applicable. Medical, evaluation and personal contact information are never public Fantasy or Draft data.

## 15. Rich City Fantasy — proposed participation code
- Fantasy is a **separate fan product**, not player/team selection, wagering or guaranteed professional exposure.
- **Initial launch must be free to enter, with no cash-equivalent mandatory stake and no paid-entry prize contests.** Any paid or prize-bearing redesign requires specialist Virginia counsel and an explicit approval gate.
- Fantasy seasons must bind to the **confirmed RCL 2027–28 official season**, not imported historic games. If no active eligible pro season exists, show a planned preview and no live contests.
- Current platform offers eight-player fantasy rosters and five position-specific starters. Official points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks and turnovers determine scoring under published season-scoped coefficients.
- The **current player-pick flow is not an exclusive fantasy draft**. Before activation, leadership must choose and enforce **either** a funded fantasy salary-cap model with transparent costs **or** an exclusive-player draft and waiver model. Do not describe a player-add button as an exclusive draft.
- Establish lineup locks, postponed game handling, stat corrections, league ties, dispute limits, commissioner recusal, anti-collusion review, abuse reporting and no nonpublic information advantage.
- Real RCL players, staff, scorekeepers and franchise operators must be treated as potentially conflicted for competitions or prizes, including any future eligibility carveouts.
- Fantasy user rules and any award terms must be published before entry and not changed retroactively.

## 16. Enforcement and operational gates
**No official 2027–28 pro season activation unless:**
- league counsel approves competition classification, contracts, privacy, insurance and fantasy compliance;
- Commissioner signs governing rules and ratifies competition format;
- each admitted club passes documented operating and financial diligence;
- facilities, officials, scheduling, backups and emergency plans are confirmed;
- official roster and draft pool, season access controls and scorebook audit pass rehearsal;
- league finance verifies a documented season funding plan and contingency;
- public marketing distinguishes prospective clubs, proposed draft order, and confirmed official games.

**No official Draft Night opening unless:** signed rules + season record + admitted teams + sufficient player pool + validated draft order + clock and failure rehearsal.

**No active Fantasy competition unless:** confirmed official season + rules, scoring and lineup lock validation + approved fair player-selection model + anti-collusion and correction procedure.
