# RCH Advertising Inventory

## Product rule

RCH advertising is native, disclosed and frequency-capped. Paid distribution can improve visibility, but it never changes REP, rankings, ratings, statistics, awards, competitive results, editorial conclusions or selection.

## Launch inventory

| Inventory | Placement key | User experience | Default frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsored Social Feed | `social-feed` | First ad after 5 organic posts; later slots every 14 organic posts | 3/day, 2/session per campaign |
| Today in RCH Partner | `network-home` | Light sponsor strip below the Today snapshot | 4/day, 2/session |
| Featured Event | `event-spotlight` | Native event promotion around Network event discovery | 3/day, 2/session |
| Community Feature | `community-feature` | Sponsor card after Community filters and before results | 3/day, 2/session |
| Search + Discover | `search-feature` | Sponsored discovery after initial people/player results and in Discover | 3/day, 2/session |
| RCH TV Partner | `media-feature` | Presenting strip between live court and Originals | 6/day, 3/session |
| RCH Runs Presenting Partner | `regional-feature` | Persistent low-interruption presenting treatment on Runs | 12/day, 8/session |

Existing secondary inventory remains available for News (`news-feature`), Weekend Guide / digest (`digest`) and Network Home campaigns.

## Delivery behavior

- The first Social Feed ad never appears before five organic posts.
- Feed slots are separated by fourteen organic posts.
- The delivery component prefers campaigns with fewer local impressions that day.
- When alternatives exist, the same advertiser is not selected immediately after itself.
- A campaign stops rendering on a browser after reaching its daily or session cap.
- An impression only counts after at least 50% of the placement becomes visible.
- Clicks continue to flow through RCH Reach campaign reporting.
- Region-targeted placements only render when the surface supplies a matching region or the campaign is statewide.

## Ad-free trust surfaces

Do not add commercial inventory to direct messages, login/signup, account settings, profile editing, REP calculation, official rankings, official statistics, official game results, checkout/payment or admin workflows.

## Creative standard

Every placement must:
1. Display a clear sponsored disclosure.
2. Use a native RCH visual treatment rather than third-party banner code.
3. Provide one clear destination and CTA.
4. Avoid deceptive ranking, editorial or basketball-credibility language.
5. Remain usable on mobile without pushing the core user task below an intrusive interstitial.
