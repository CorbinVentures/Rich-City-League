# Appendix A — proposed on-court rules and game-operations standard
**Status:** Draft for commissioner, head referee, venue and finance review — NOT adopted.  
**Applies only after signed competition rulebook and season-specific appendix are published.**

## Recommended rules authority
For the 2027–28 adult competition, **propose the FIBA Official Basketball Rules 2026 (v1.1; effective October 1, 2026)**, plus expressly approved RCL administrative regulations. Source: https://about.fiba.basketball/en/services/resource-hub/downloads

**Important 2026 update:** FIBA's 2026 edition distinguishes technical-foul categories and introduces `disruptive` and `flagrant` foul terminology in place of the former unsportsmanlike-foul system. Referee training, Scorebook event types, disciplinary reports and official rule citations must match the adopted edition. Source: https://about.fiba.basketball/en/news/fiba-official-basketball-rules-2026-to-take-effect-october-1

If RCL cannot supply an accurate game clock, official 24-second shot clock, trained table crew, suitable facility markings and qualified referees at **every** venue, it must either obtain the equipment and personnel or **formally choose a different complete rules code** (such as an appropriately licensed/current NFHS code) before the season. Do not casually combine FIBA timing with NFHS fouls and administrative rules. An improvised hybrid is not an acceptable official professional league default.

## Proposed official adult game profile (subject to approved FIBA 2026 code)
- Five players per side on the court; game may only start or continue with the legally required minimum under the adopted rules.
- Four **10-minute regulation quarters** with the timing, quarter breaks and halftime required by the adopted code.
- Tie at the end of regulation: **five-minute overtime periods** until a winner is determined under the adopted code.
- Use the adopted code's **24-second shot clock**, reset and possession procedures. Table operator must demonstrate proficiency before being scheduled.
- All timeouts, fouls, disqualifications, throw-ins, free throws, substitutions, team foul penalties, replay availability, appealability, and score-correction mechanics follow the selected *current* official rulebook unless the finalized RCL season appendix explicitly adopts a permitted local modification.
- No unauthorised variation by venue or home team. A tournament/event appendix must distinguish genuine exhibition rules from official league play.

## Officials and staffing
- Minimum pregame requirements: qualified officiating crew, official scorer, independent scoreboard/game-clock operator, independent shot-clock operator under FIBA play, site/game director, access to first aid/AED and a documented emergency action plan.
- Separate staff must approve official game statistics; a scorer must not silently overwrite disputed incidents or invent player-level stats.
- Verify equipment and roster sheets 45 minutes before scheduled tipoff (illustrative operations target, not a published requirement).
- The referee-in-chief, table officials and team representatives sign the official game report before finalization.

## Officials' briefing and authority
- Publish edition number and local modifications in referee contracts and team packet.
- Match referee training to updated 2026 FIBA foul classifications.
- Head referee decides real-time on-court rule interpretation; league competition office handles administrative eligibility and postgame protests.
- Inaccurate game clock, shot clock or scorekeeper data must be treated as officiating and competition integrity issues, not production glitches.

## Game cancellation, delays and hardship
- Before any fixture, publish minimum player count, grace period, forfeit conditions, postponement triggers, replay criteria, refund handling and responsibility for venue/travel expenses.
- If an athlete has a suspected concussion or serious injury, stop play and follow the emergency protocol. No return solely on coach permission.
- Unsafe courts, missing medical response, unqualified officials or broken essential timing equipment may stop official play; escalation goes to the game director and league competition office.

## Approval checklist
- [ ] Commissioner signs competition format and selected rules edition
- [ ] Head of Officiating validates personnel availability and training
- [ ] Every home venue passes clock/shot-clock, safety and equipment inspections
- [ ] Scorebook tested against approved 2026 foul categories and periods
- [ ] Clubs receive identical rulebook PDF and sign receipt
- [ ] All exceptions and local modifications are published with effective dates
- [ ] Final PDF/canonical URL and change log are archived before first official game

**Do not turn on official fixtures until the entire rule authority, staffing and equipment plan is technically and operationally feasible.**
